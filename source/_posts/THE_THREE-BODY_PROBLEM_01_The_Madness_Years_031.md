<!-- 句子结构拆解 -->
<section data-role="paragraph" class="_myeditor">
	<section style="background-color: #F4ECF7; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">
		<h3 style="color: #8E44AD; font-size: 17px;">
			🔍 句子结构拆解与讲解
		</h3>
		<section style="background-color: white; padding: 15px; border-radius: 5px;">
			<p><strong>第一句：</strong><em>But such desirable victims had grown scarce.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>状语：</strong>But（轻微转折，“然而”）</li>
				<li><strong>主语：</strong>such desirable victims（“如此抢手的受害者”）</li>
				<li><strong>谓语：</strong>had grown scarce（“变得稀缺”）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>“had grown” 过去完成时，强调“已经变得”，与上文“过剩”形成反差；desirable 含“抢手、合意”之意，暗示红卫兵对“猎物”的猎奇标准。</p>

			<p><strong>第二句：</strong><em>In Tsinghua there was probably only one left.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>地点状语：</strong>In Tsinghua（“在清华”）</li>
				<li><strong>主语：</strong>there（存在句引导词）</li>
				<li><strong>谓语：</strong>was（存在句系动词）</li>
				<li><strong>表语：</strong>probably only one left（“大概只剩一个”）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>存在句型简化场景，probably 弱化绝对性，增强口语感；only one left 用后置形容词 left，表“剩余”，简洁有力。</p>

			<p><strong>第三句：</strong><em>Because he was so rare, he was reserved for the very end of the struggle session.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>原因状语从句：</strong>Because he was so rare（“因为他如此稀有”）</li>
				<li><strong>主句：</strong>he was reserved for the very end...（“被留到批斗会的最末尾”）</li>
				<li><strong>状语：</strong>for the very end of the struggle session（“作为压轴”）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>“was reserved” 被动语态，突出“被安排、被预留”；very 置于 end 前，表示“正是、最”，强化压轴仪式感。整句形成“稀缺→压轴”因果链，为后文“终极批斗”埋伏笔。</p>

			<p><strong>修辞与情感：</strong></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>稀缺→压轴：</strong>数量递减→价值递增，形成“最后一击”期待。</li>
				<li><strong>地点具体化：</strong>Tsinghua 点名顶级学府，增强真实感与象征性。</li>
				<li><strong>被动语态：</strong>was reserved 弱化施事者，突出“猎物”被安排的命运。</li>
			</ul>
		</section>
	</section>
</section>

<!-- 拓展学习 -->
<section data-role="paragraph" class="_myeditor">
	<section style="background-color: #E8F8F5; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">
		<h3 style="color: #148F77; font-size: 17px;">
			💡 拓展学习
		</h3>
		<p style="color: #0E6251;">
			1. “had grown scarce” 可替换为 had become rare / had dwindled to a handful，但 grown 带“逐渐”意味，更合语境。<br>
			2. “only one left” 为口语高频表达，同类：only two remaining / just one survivor，可整理“只剩”表达矩阵。<br>
			3. “was reserved for” 被动语态，可升级为主动：they reserved him for...，但被动更突出“猎物”无助。<br>
			4. “the very end” 中 very 为强调副词，同类：the very beginning / the very top，可整理“very+名词”强调结构。<br>
			5. 背景知识：1968 年清华“百日大武斗”后，校内“牛鬼蛇神”已由 200 余人减至不足 10 人，与“只剩一个”高度吻合。<br>
			6. 写作仿用：描写“稀缺压轴”可用 “By the final round, only one contestant remained, and his rarity earned him the spotlight reserved for the grand finale.”<br>
			7. 口语拓展：用 “down to just one” 替代 only one left，更口语化，例：We’re down to just one cookie.<br>
			8. 地名发音：Tsinghua 威妥玛拼音，现代拼音为 Qinghua，阅读英文文献时需注意拼写与发音差异。
		</p>
