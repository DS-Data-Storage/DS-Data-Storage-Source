<!-- 句子结构拆解 -->
<section data-role="paragraph" class="_myeditor">
	<section style="background-color: #F4ECF7; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">
		<h3 style="color: #8E44AD; font-size: 17px;">
			🔍 句子结构拆解与讲解
		</h3>
		<section style="background-color: white; padding: 15px; border-radius: 5px;">
			<p><strong>第一句：</strong><em>They began to really believe that they were guilty, to see how they had harmed the great cause of the revolution.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>主语：</strong>They（指“知识分子”）</li>
				<li><strong>谓语：</strong>began to really believe...to see...（“开始真正相信……开始明白……”）</li>
				<li><strong>宾语从句1：</strong>that they were guilty（“他们是有罪的”）</li>
				<li><strong>宾语从句2：</strong>how they had harmed the great cause of the revolution（“自己如何危害了革命伟大事业”）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>“began to + 动词” 表示心理过程的起点；really 前置强调“由衷”相信，体现自我洗脑的深度。how 引导的宾语从句用疑问副词，把“危害方式”具体化，增强忏悔的细腻度。</p>

			<p><strong>第二句：</strong><em>They cried, and their repentance was far deeper and more sincere than that of those Monsters and Demons who were not intellectuals.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>分句1：</strong>They cried（“他们哭了”）</li>
				<li><strong>分句2：</strong>their repentance was far deeper and more sincere...（“他们的悔悟远为深刻与真诚”）</li>
				<li><strong>比较对象：</strong>than that of those Monsters and Demons...（“比那些非知识分子的牛鬼蛇神”）</li>
				<li><strong>定语从句：</strong>who were not intellectuals（“不是知识分子的”）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>“far + 比较级” 加强程度；that 替代不可数名词 repentance，避免重复。引号外的 Monsters and Demons 为文革术语，加引号示贬义，与 intellectuals 形成身份对立，突出“知识分子忏悔更真诚”的反讽。</p>

			<p><strong>修辞与情感：</strong></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>递进结构：</strong>believe → see → cried → repentance，完成“认知—情感—行动”的忏悔链。</li>
				<li><strong>对比反讽：</strong>知识分子 vs 牛鬼蛇神，前者“更真诚”反而更显荒诞，批判力度更强。</li>
				<li><strong>程度副词：</strong>really / far deeper / more sincere 层层升级，制造情感高潮。</li>
			</ul>
		</section>
	</section>
</section>

<!-- 拓展学习 -->
<section data-role="paragraph" class="_myeditor">
	<section style="background-color: #E8F8F5; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">
		<h3 style="color: #148F77; font-size: 17px;">
			💡 拓展学习
		</h3>
		<p style="color: #0E6251;">
			1. “began to + 动词” 可升级为 “started to / commenced to”，但 began 最自然；really 置于 to 前强调“由衷”，位置不可后移。<br>
			2. “the great cause of the revolution” 为文革高频套语，英文保留定冠词 the，示特指，写作中可替换为 the great undertaking / the noble mission。<br>
			3. “far + 比较级” 结构可替换为 way / much / significantly + 比较级，口语中 way 更常见，书面语推荐 far。<br>
			4. “that of those Monsters...” 中 that 替代不可数名词 repentance，同类用法：that of the previous generation / that of ordinary people。<br>
			5. 身份标签对比：intellectuals vs Monsters and Demons，可整理文革英文词汇表：reactionaries / bourgeois elements / capitalist roaders 等。<br>
			6. 背景知识：1969 年“清理阶级队伍”后期，北京高校出现“知识分子痛哭请罪”现象，本句为真实写照。<br>
			7. 写作仿用：描写“自我否定”可用 “They began to genuinely believe that they were the root of all evil, their remorse far more acute than that of the bystanders who had merely been swept along.”<br>
			8. 口语拓展：用 “way more sincere” 替代 far more sincere，可提高口语地道度，但正式文本仍推荐 far。
		</p>
