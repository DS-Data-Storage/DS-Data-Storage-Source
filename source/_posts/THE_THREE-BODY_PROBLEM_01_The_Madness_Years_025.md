<!-- 句子结构拆解 -->
<section data-role="paragraph" class="_myeditor">
	<section style="background-color: #F4ECF7; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">
		<h3 style="color: #8E44AD; font-size: 17px;">
			🔍 句子结构拆解与讲解
		</h3>
		<section style="background-color: white; padding: 15px; border-radius: 5px;">
			<p><strong>第一句：</strong><em>Over a period of forty days, in Beijing alone, more than seventeen hundred victims of struggle sessions were beaten to death.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>时间状语：</strong>Over a period of forty days（在四十天的时间里）</li>
				<li><strong>地点状语：</strong>in Beijing alone（仅在北京一地，“alone”强调数量之惊人）</li>
				<li><strong>主语：</strong>more than seventeen hundred victims of struggle sessions（一千七百多名批斗会受害者）</li>
				<li><strong>谓语：</strong>were beaten to death（被殴打致死，被动语态突出受害者的无力与残酷）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>该句通过“alone”与“more than”形成强烈对比，凸显暴行规模之大；被动语态弱化施暴者，突出受害者的悲惨命运。</p>

			<p><strong>第二句：</strong><em>Many others picked an easier path to avoid the madness: Lao She, Wu Han, Jian Bozan, Fu Lei, Zhao Jiuzhang, Yi Qun, Wen Jie, Hai Mo, and other once-respected intellectuals had all chosen to end their lives.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>主句主语：</strong>Many others（许多其他人）</li>
				<li><strong>谓语：</strong>picked（选择了）</li>
				<li><strong>宾语：</strong>an easier path（一条更“轻松”的路，反语修辞）</li>
				<li><strong>目的状语：</strong>to avoid the madness（为了避免疯狂）</li>
				<li><strong>同位语列举：</strong>Lao She... and other once-respected intellectuals（具体列出8位知识分子姓名，增强真实感与震撼力）</li>
				<li><strong>同位语谓语：</strong>had all chosen to end their lives（都选择了结束生命，过去完成时强调“在疯狂来临之前”已做出决定）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>冒号后的列举形成强烈视觉与情感冲击，“once-respected”与“end their lives”形成巨大反差，揭示时代对知识与尊严的摧残。</p>

			<p><strong>修辞手法：</strong></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>反语：</strong>“an easier path”实为自杀，表面“轻松”，实则绝望。</li>
				<li><strong>排比：</strong>连续列举8位人名，节奏急促，如泣如诉。</li>
				<li><strong>对比：</strong>“once-respected”与“beaten to death / end their lives”形成荣耀与毁灭的极端对照。</li>
			</ul>
		</section>
	</section>
</section>

<!-- 拓展学习 -->
<section data-role="paragraph" class="_myeditor">
	<section style="background-color: #E8F8F5; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">
		<h3 style="color: #148F77; font-size: 17px;">
			💡 拓展学习
		</h3>
		<p style="color: #0E6251;">
			1. “struggle session”为文革特有词汇，直译“批斗会”，英文语境下需加引号或注释，避免读者误解为普通“斗争”。<br>
			2. “beaten to death”为常见新闻英语表达，可替换为“fatally assaulted”“killed by bludgeoning”等，但情感强度递减。<br>
			3. “once-respected”中的“once”表示“曾经”，含“今非昔比”之意，类似结构有“once-thriving”“once-prosperous”。<br>
			4. 中文人名拼音在英文中保持“姓+名”顺序，朗读时需注意声调差异，如“Lao She”第三声+第一声。<br>
			5. 被动语态“were beaten to death”可改写为主动语态“mobs beat...to death”以突出施暴者，但原文刻意弱化施暴者，强调受害者命运。<br>
			6. 冒号后的列举属于“appositive list”，在写作中可用于增强细节与情感冲击，适合用于演讲或纪实文本。<br>
			7. 数字表达：英文“seventeen hundred”比“one thousand seven hundred”更简洁，适合新闻或文学语境。<br>
			8. 反语修辞“an easier path”可作为写作技巧，用于描写“看似解脱实则绝望”的情境，增强批判力度。
		</p>
	</section>
</section>
