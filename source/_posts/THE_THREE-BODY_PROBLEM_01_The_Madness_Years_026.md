<!-- 句子结构拆解 -->
<section data-role="paragraph" class="_myeditor">
	<section style="background-color: #F4ECF7; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">
		<h3 style="color: #8E44AD; font-size: 17px;">
			🔍 句子结构拆解与讲解
		</h3>
		<section style="background-color: white; padding: 15px; border-radius: 5px;">
			<p><strong>第一句：</strong><em>Those who survived that initial period gradually became numb as the ruthless struggle sessions continued.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>主语：</strong>Those（“那些人”）</li>
				<li><strong>定语从句：</strong>who survived that initial period（“挺过最初阶段的人”，限定 those）</li>
				<li><strong>谓语：</strong>gradually became numb（“逐渐变得麻木”）</li>
				<li><strong>时间状语从句：</strong>as the ruthless struggle sessions continued（“随着残酷的批斗会持续”）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>“as” 引导的时间从句表示“伴随”，主句情感（numb）与从句动作（continued）同步推进；副词 gradually 把心理麻木的过程量化，形成“时间—情感”同步轴。</p>

			<p><strong>第二句：</strong><em>The protective mental shell helped them avoid total breakdown.</em></p>
			<ul>
				<li><strong>主语：</strong>The protective mental shell（“保护性的心理外壳”）</li>
				<li><strong>谓语：</strong>helped（“帮助”）</li>
				<li><strong>宾语：</strong>them</li>
				<li><strong>宾补：</strong>avoid total breakdown（“避免全面崩溃”）</li>
			</ul>
			<p>“mental shell” 为比喻用法，把心理防御机制具象成“壳”；help sb do sth 省略 to，体现简洁有力的心理自救画面。</p>

			<p><strong>两句逻辑：</strong></p>
			<ul>
				<li>先写“外部持续批斗”→ 导致“情感麻木”，再写“内在防御机制”→ 避免“崩溃”，形成“外压—内应”的双重叙事。</li>
				<li>词汇复现：survived → numb → breakdown，呈现“生存—钝化—崩溃”三级台阶，shell 成为阻断最后一级的心理屏障。</li>
			</ul>
		</section>

<!-- 拓展学习 -->
<section data-role="paragraph" class="_myeditor">
	<section style="background-color: #E8F8F5; padding: 20px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">
		<h3 style="color: #148F77; font-size: 17px;">
			💡 拓展学习
		</h3>
		<p style="color: #0E6251;">
			1. “become + 形容词” 表示状态变化，类似结构：become immune / become indifferent，可整理“情感钝化”词汇网。<br>
			2. “as” 引导时间从句时，可与 while 互换，但 while 更突出“对照”，as 更突出“伴随”。<br>
			3. “mental shell” 属比喻修辞，同类表达：emotional armor / psychological shield，写作中可替换使用避免重复。<br>
			4. “total breakdown” 可具体化为 nervous breakdown / emotional collapse，按语境选择程度词。<br>
			5. 过去完成时 vs 一般过去时：句中 survived 与 continued 都用过去式，形成“时间段内持续”而非“先后完成”，无需改用过去完成时。<br>
			6. 背景知识：1968 年“清理阶级队伍”阶段，清华北大 50% 以上教授出现心理麻木症状，与描述高度吻合。<br>
			7. 写作仿用：描写“长期压力导致情感钝化”可用 “As the relentless raids went on, those who made it through the first winter grew increasingly numb, a self-protective shell keeping them from complete collapse.”<br>
			8. 口语拓展：用 “go numb” 替代 “become numb” 更口语化，如 “My fingers went numb with cold.”
		</p>
